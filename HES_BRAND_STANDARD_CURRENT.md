## Owner supersession instruction — 21 September 2026

Version 2.8 is the **single controlling HES brand standard for active use in this repository**. It supersedes **all earlier HES brand standards, brand guides, colour instructions, contact rules, templates and conflicting brand-control notes** for active production and deployment.

Earlier standards may remain in the repository or archive for audit/history only. They are **not controlling**. Where any earlier instruction, asset, template, code treatment, AI-generated output, supplier material or repository note conflicts with Version 2.8, **Version 2.8 and this owner supersession instruction prevail**.

No later brand standard may replace Version 2.8 unless there is explicit written approval from the HES Brand Owner / Management.

## Digital, website and application controls

All HES-owned or HES-authorised digital interfaces must use the approved HES identity system and must preserve functional, security and accessibility requirements.

- Responsive HES design system with approved logo, palette, typography, imagery, CTA and contact details.
- Test desktop and mobile layouts, links, forms, accessibility, privacy, security and performance before production release.
- Client, supplier and admin portals must use role-appropriate interfaces, protected permissions, controlled data visibility, audit trails and environment separation.
- Never expose credentials, tokens, secrets or privileged backend values in the frontend or interface.
- Landing pages and funnels must define scope, exclusions, response expectations, third-party dependencies and approvals before collecting leads or payments.
- E-commerce/payment pages must present accurate pricing, tax, delivery, return and legal information.
- Where PayFast is active, use the approved payment-stage wording only at the payment/checkout stage: **“Payments are processed via PayFast and administered by Hotel Equipment Suppliers (HES). For further information, please contact Admin.”**
- Do not place the PayFast wording generally in site footers or where PayFast is inactive.

## Social and campaign application

- Facebook: concise operational benefit; normally 1–2 relevant hashtags.
- Instagram feed default: **1080 × 1350 px (4:5)** with key content in the central safe area; 3–5 relevant hashtags including `#HospitalityConsidered`.
- LinkedIn: approved business-led layout and caption; normally 3–5 relevant hashtags.
- Universal organic feed asset when one visual must serve Facebook, Instagram and LinkedIn: **1080 × 1350 px (4:5)**, with logo, headline, CTA and contact details kept inside the safe area and previewed on all three platforms.
- Stories/Reels: create a separate **1080 × 1920 px (9:16)** asset; do not blindly crop a feed advert.
- Platform-specific formats take precedence where placement materially affects crop, hierarchy or CTA.
- If platform/placement is unclear and materially affects format, clarify before final production.

## Commercial, content and AI controls

- Use UK English and a professional hospitality/procurement-led tone.
- Lead with the buyer requirement, operational benefit or project outcome.
- Do not publish unsupported claims, guaranteed savings, guaranteed delivery dates, uncontrolled availability statements or unintended warranties.
- Protect client confidentiality, supplier commercial information, personal data and project permissions.
- Use rights-cleared, credible and culturally appropriate hospitality imagery.
- AI-generated imagery may support conceptual communication but may not recreate the HES logo or imply that a conceptual item is an actual supplied product or completed HES project.
- AI-assisted copy is a draft until facts, claims, dates, prices, names, links, legal wording and tone are reviewed and approved.
- Disable or review automatic platform enhancements that crop, recolour, animate, rewrite or extend HES assets where they could affect identity, factual accuracy or product representation.

## Release governance

Every published or deployed HES item must pass the following control gates where applicable:

1. **Brief:** platform, placement, audience, market, objective and required action confirmed.
2. **Source:** official logo, template, imagery, product data, contact details and latest brand standard confirmed.
3. **Brand:** logo, palette, typography, hierarchy, imagery and signature compliant.
4. **Commercial:** price, currency, tax, availability, lead time, scope, claims, exclusions and CTA supportable.
5. **Rights and risk:** privacy, consent, licensing, confidentiality, legal wording and client/supplier permissions addressed.
6. **Technical:** actual placement/device/file previewed for crop, contrast, links, forms, captions, resolution, accessibility and security.
7. **Release:** final asset/copy, version, approver, date, destination and any budget authority recorded.
